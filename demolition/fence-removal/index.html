Questions

What people ask before they call

Do you haul the old fence away?

Yes. Same crew, same truck. Take down and haul away is one booking.

What about the posts?

Tell us on the call what the posts are set in and we'll tell you how we handle them.

Are you licensed?

Yes. CT demolition license dmcr.003466, Home Improvement Contractor HIC.0702580, New Home Construction Contractor NHC.0018516, and EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm NAT-F325293-1.

How is fence removal priced?

By the length and the material. You get a firm price before we start.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

We can give you a good idea on the call based on what you describe. The firm price comes when the crew sees it, and you approve it before anything moves.

More questions (3)
Do I have to be home?

Not always. If we can get to everything, tell us on the call and we'll work out how you see and approve the price before we start.

Can you remove just part of a fence?

Yes. One section, one side of the yard, or the whole run.

Do you remove chain link and vinyl fences?

Yes. Wood, vinyl, chain link, picket and split rail.
