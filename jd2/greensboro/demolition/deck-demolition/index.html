Questions people ask before they call

Do you remove the concrete footings?

We pull the posts. Concrete footings are an add-on, and many people keep them for a rebuild. Tell us when you book.

What has to happen before you start?

Any water, gas or electric connected to what's coming out has to be shut off and capped by a licensed plumber or electrician first. We don't do utility work, and we don't start until it's confirmed off.

How long does it take?

A standard attached deck usually takes two to four hours. Bigger or multi level decks take longer.

How is demolition priced?

On site, after we walk the job. It depends on the size, materials, access and how much there is to haul. You get one firm price before we start, and it only changes if the scope does.

Do you haul it all away?

Yes. Every demolition job includes the haul away. The same crew tears it down, loads it and takes it with us. No dumpster and no separate hauler.
