Questions people ask before they call

Something else on your mind? Call and ask.
☎ (919) 372-0602

Can you demo just the shower?

Yes. Partial bathroom demo is common. You tell us the scope and we stay in that zone.

Do you turn off the water?

No. We're a demo and hauling crew, not plumbers. A plumber needs to cap the supply lines and disconnect the drains before we start.

What if there is mold?

If we see obvious mold, we'll tell you before going further. Mold remediation isn't something we do.

How is demolition priced?

On site, after we walk the job. It depends on the size, materials, access and how much there is to haul. You get one firm price before we start, and it only changes if the scope does.

Do you haul it all away?

Yes. Every demolition job includes the haul away. The same crew tears it down, loads it and takes it with us. No dumpster and no separate hauler.

More questions (1)
What about asbestos in an older home?

We don't do asbestos work. If a certified inspector or abatement company is needed, that happens before we start. Tell us the age of the home when you call.
