Questions people ask

Before you call

How soon can you come?

Same-day is often available if you call before 3pm. After that we'll get you on the schedule for the next opening. You get a 2 hour arrival window, not an all day wait, and rescheduling is easy.

How does pricing work?

It's based on how much room your stuff takes up in the truck. The crew sees it, gives you a firm price, and you approve it before anything is lifted. No surprises at the end.

Do I have to carry anything out?

No. Basement, attic, upstairs bedroom or the back of the yard, the crew goes and gets it. You just point.

Where does it all go?

Anything reusable goes to local donation centers when they'll take it, metals and electronics go to facilities that accept them, and the rest is disposed of properly.

Is it just one item? Is that worth a call?

Yes. One couch or one fridge is a normal call for us.
